Scoring

Bayesian confidence scoring

Every pattern carries a Beta distribution confidence score that updates with real-world outcomes.

confidence = α / (α + β)

Beta(alpha, beta) distribution — alpha tracks successes, beta tracks failures

Archival
Active
Promotion
0.0 0.1 0.8 1.0
Tier 1: Low Conversion
Exposed ≥5 times with <10% conversion. Alpha decay applied to reduce confidence over time.
Tier 2: High Exposure
Exposed >20 times. Very gentle alpha decay to prevent old winners from dominating forever.
Tier 3: Archival
Never-exposed patterns older than 30 days, or ≥10 exposures with <5% conversion and confidence <0.1.

Graceful Degradation

Multi-tier fallback chain ensures the system never fully stops:

  • Gemini 2.5 Flash (primary) — cheapest capable model
  • Vercel AI Gateway — automatic provider failover
  • Local MiniLM embeddings — zero-cost vector generation
  • SQLite fallback — works fully offline if cloud is down
  • Skip pipeline — trajectories queue until daemon recovers
